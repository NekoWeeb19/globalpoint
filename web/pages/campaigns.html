Yearly campaigns

Pick seasonal login bonuses, free pulls, stamina and reward campaigns that come back every year on the same dates (month, day and time), after their original run. Their original run stays as it was. Times are in UTC, on the server's calendar.

The game picks up a change the next time it logs in: close and reopen it, and it downloads the new dates.

    Campaigns marked Both apps are in both games' data and repeat in both the global and the CN app. Those marked CN only are the CN game's own and only show in the CN app. Stamina and reward campaigns below are the shared ones (both apps).

    Seasonal login bonuses

    Each year's run starts again at day 1. Anniversary, collab and story bonuses aren't listed: they stay as they are.

      Seasonal free pulls

        Stamina campaigns

        Each one ran once in the game. Pick one to bring it back every year on the same dates. A year where another of the game's stamina campaigns is on then is skipped, and two that would be on at the same time can't both repeat. The game shows and charges the lower stamina itself.

          Reward campaigns

          More items, EXP or mana from quests. Each one ran once in the game; the same rules as stamina campaigns apply, checked against other reward campaigns.
