Mail

Gifts that arrive in the in-game mailbox at a set time, once or again and again, like the game's own maintenance and event gifts. Times are the server's (UTC), so moving the server time moves them too. Each mail can be claimed for 30 days after it arrives, then it's gone.

The never-ending mails (Bread, Mana, EXP) are on the Dashboard tab. Players > Give sends a one-off mail right away.
